MODEL NOTES AND SOURCES

SCOPE
I modelled 24 classrooms: 12 regular-use and 12 extended-use rooms.
Each has four 4 x 18 W fluorescent fixtures and one 235 W smartboard.
The classroom inventory and timetable come from my school. Our classrooms
have no separate computers.

The study covers lighting and smartboards. Heating, cooling, ballast losses,
standby and controller consumption are excluded.

LOADS AND SENSOR INPUT
The supply is 230 V RMS, 50 Hz. Each fixture is represented by 734.7 ohms
and the smartboard by 225.10 ohms. These loads represent nominal active power,
not device internals or measured average consumption.

The daylight divider uses a 5 V supply, a 10k/100k upper resistance for
bright/dark conditions, a 47k lower resistance and a 2.5 V threshold.
It is not calibrated to illuminance in lux.

MAIN COMPARISON
The modelled day is 08:00-17:30. Manual lights are on 08:00-09:00 and
13:45-17:30: half the observation window. Daylight is assumed sufficient
09:00-15:00. These are scenario assumptions, not measured patterns.

Regular rooms become empty at 16:05; extended rooms at 17:00. Some students
remain during breaks, so occupancy continues through them. Smartboard demand
follows lessons in both strategies; main-case savings therefore come from
lighting. The full timetable is represented in the command files.

Automatic control uses perfect detection and a 180-second switch-off delay.
Both experiments begin occupied with detection active. Empty-start behaviour
is outside this configuration.

SENSOR-GAP TEST
This separate test assumes continuous darkness and detection gaps of
20, 120, 240 and 420 seconds while the room remains occupied. I compare
switch-off delays of 0, 60, 180, 300 and 600 seconds.

Longer delays reduce unwanted interruptions but increase energy use after
departure. The gaps are test inputs, not measured sensor failure rates.

MEASUREMENTS
TIME_SCALE=60 maps 570 simulation seconds to 9.5 real hours. Schedules,
timers and energy calculations use this scale factor; AC remains 50 Hz
in simulation time.

E_load_Wh: total energy delivered by the load supply.
E_empty_Wh: energy consumed while the classroom is empty.
T_light_off_s: time lighting is off despite occupancy and darkness.
T_board_off_s: time the board is off despite occupancy and demand.

SCHOOL-SCALE PROJECTIONS
Totals combine 12 rooms of each schedule type. Monthly and yearly estimates
repeat the same scenario for 20 and 180 school days, without seasonal variation.
Prices of 3, 5 and 7 TRY/kWh are illustrative.
Cost estimates exclude equipment, installation and maintenance.
